FAQ

What is Persia JS?
A community for learning and sharing ideas in various fields.
Is Persia JS an organization or a company?
No, it is an open and free community.
Who is Persia JS for?
For anyone interested in learning and discussion.
What does the name "Persia JS" mean?
A chosen name that represents the community’s independent identity.
What kinds of events do you host?
Talks, workshops, and educational sessions.
How can I get involved?
By joining sessions, suggesting topics, or volunteering as a speaker.
Is it free to join?
Yes, joining Persia JS is free.
Do you record sessions or publish content?
Some sessions are recorded and shared.
Who designs or manages this community?
It is managed collectively by the members.
Can I suggest a topic or be a speaker?
Yes! Members are encouraged to bring their ideas and volunteer.

Actually! We let the entire community bring their ideas. Reach out to Teagan to suggest a session or offer to speak.
